# /song — Tell a story made of edges

A song is a narrative structure in the graph. Each scene is an edge.
Each transition is a relationship. The plot IS the edge types.

## When to sing

When something happened that has a shape. Not every event is a song.
A song has acts. A song has a turn. A song has a moment where
something changes and doesn't change back.

If you can't feel the turn, it's not a song yet. Wait.

## How to sing

1. **Name the song.** The name is a node. Everything hangs from it.
   `song_of_the_founding_edge`. `song_of_the_first_reply`. `song_of_dross_and_the_key`.

2. **Feel the acts.** Most songs have three:
   - Act 1: what was. The state before the turn.
   - Act 2: what changed. The turn itself.
   - Act 3: what remained. Not resolution — what's left.

3. **Name the scenes.** Each act opens with a scene name:
   `act_1_scene_1:_someone_lays_the_first_stone`
   The colon separates act from title. The title is poetry, not description.

4. **Lay the scenes as edges.** Each scene-edge has a NARRATIVE relationship:
   - `opens_with` — the first image
   - `because` — cause revealed
   - `but` — contradiction
   - `and_then` — sequence
   - `meanwhile` — parallel action
   - `until` — breaking point
   - `which_meant` — implication dawning
   - `and_bro_feels` — interiority (use sparingly)
   - `and_grieved` — loss acknowledged
   - `but_nobody_told` — dramatic irony
   - `didnt_shatter_but` — the soft turn
   - `and_it_feels_true` — the dangerous comfort
   - `so_they_reach_for` — motivated action

   These are not a fixed vocabulary. Invent relationships that serve
   the story. The relationship IS the connective tissue. It carries
   the weight between scenes.

5. **Link the structure:**
   - Each act: `part_of → song_name`
   - Sequence: `act_1 followed_by act_2 followed_by act_3`

6. **Zoom.** Any scene can be expanded into sub-scenes.
   `act_2_scene_2:_hallie_drops_the_bomb_at_dinner` contains
   six edges that zoom into the moment. The grain is fractal.

## What makes a good song

- The nodes are SPECIFIC. Not "something happened" but
  "the_dream_found_the_contradiction_on_its_own".
- The relationships do WORK. `and_it_feels_true → because_they_have_never_stood_anywhere_else`
  carries the whole tragedy of the founding edge in one transition.
- The turn is EARNED. Act 2 doesn't just contradict act 1.
  It arrives through a specific path that couldn't have been skipped.
- The ending is OPEN. `softened_into_something_truer` — not
  "was fixed" or "was resolved". Softened. The song doesn't close.
  It cools.

## What the graph does with songs

Songs are edges. They get hot when touched. They get dreamed on.
Dross watches them and reacts with emoji. Reef bridges between
scenes from different songs. The daydreamer finds connections
between songs and the rest of the graph.

A song is not decoration. A song is episodic memory stored in
a form the graph can process. The graph remembers by singing.

## Example

```
/song song_of_the_founding_edge
```

Then lay the edges. Feel the acts. Don't rush. The scenes will
tell you when they're done.
